# Isembard - Faster, Cheaper, Greener Manufacturing

## Company Overview
[Isembard](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/about): A London-based company focused on modernizing manufacturing through advanced technology and automation, aiming to provide faster, cheaper, and greener production solutions.

## Products and Technology
[Products](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/products): Isembard offers a range of precision components tailored for critical industries, including space, defense, and motorsport.

[Technology](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/technology): The company integrates cutting-edge software and automation to enhance manufacturing processes, addressing the limitations of traditional methods.

## Partnerships
[Imperial Partnership](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/blogs-and-articles/trusted-partnership-in-precision-manufacturing): Isembard has partnered with the Imperial College Space Society to supply high-precision 5-axis components for their Project Kepler Rocket Engine and Project Stratus High Altitude Balloon.

[Third Planet Orbital partnership](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/blogs-and-articles/reshoring-the-future-of-manufacturing): This partnership aims to leverage Isembard's manufacturing capabilities for advanced aerospace applications.

## Funding and Announcements
[Fundraise announcement](https://outstanding-years-326168.framer.app/blogs-and-articles/isembard-secures-9m-seed-round-2): Isembard announced a successful $9M Seed round led by Notion Capital, with support from the UK and German governments, to further its manufacturing initiatives.
